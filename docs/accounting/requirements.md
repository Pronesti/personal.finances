# Accounting migration requirements

Accepted requirements from the Tarjetas documentation and domain interview, recorded on 2026-10-25. This is the concise handoff for future accounting work. [The decision record](../current-state/accounting-questions.md) retains the numbered answers and unresolved choices; [the current-state guide](../current-state/README.md) describes the existing application. Documentation and research do not authorize implementation, data migration, deployment, commits, or publishing.

The initial delivery was a verified backup and planning only. The user later authorized implementation of the approved plans. The [incremental implementation plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-accounting-architecture.md) maps these requirements to concrete task groups, checks and operational gates; [implementation status](implementation-status.md) distinguishes implemented code, verification, remaining acceptance and live cutover.

## Scope and sequence

1. Migrate the existing card and supermarket-receipt capabilities into a double-entry accounting architecture. Preserve the user-visible features and evidence they need; retaining implementation code is optional.
2. Add bank accounts and transfers after those features are migrated. Keep extension points for purchase → receipt evidence → card liability → actual payment account without adding bank workflows to the first release.
3. Cover one person's finances with explicit distinct account/card identities. Household splits, business books, investments, and complete wealth-management features are outside the initial scope.
4. Support hosting and PostgreSQL. No application login is required for now. Hosting provider, network exposure, document storage, deployment details, and OCR engine have not been selected.

Use [all 33 feature capabilities](../current-state/features.md), [current workflows](../current-state/data-and-workflows.md), and [preservation scenarios](../current-state/preservation.md) as the coverage baseline. Explain explicitly agreed corrections to current approximations/defects instead of requiring exact reproduction of every old number. Unclassified or unreconciled data must remain visible, not disappear to make reports look complete.

## Financial and source distinctions

- Keep issuer facts, receipt facts, classification decisions, accounting entries, and derived forecasts/report values distinguishable and traceable.
- A receipt and billed charge can describe the same purchase. Linking evidence or allocating items must not add another purchase or repeat the full purchase against every installment.
- Preserve native ARS/USD amounts and available actual rate evidence. Balance native currencies independently in the initial card migration; keep IPC/MEP restatement as separate reporting lenses. Functional valuation and cross-currency settlement policies are deferred until before bank conversions.
- Imported statement payments reduce the card liability against an explicit unresolved payment-source clearing role until bank evidence identifies the origin. They are not income or reduced purchase spending; do not invent a bank account.
- Preserve differing receipt/issuer amounts and require an explained reconciliation. Do not silently overwrite either source or fabricate cents to force a match.
- Q8 accepts the [installment policy](installments.md): recognize one evidenced purchase and its full known principal once, using the appropriate expense/asset/prepayment account. Maintain billed and unbilled liability subaccounts per card and native currency. Billing moves principal between them without new purchase expense or increased total debt; actual repayments reduce debt. Preserve purchase-period, billed-cycle, payment and future-commitment views without double counting. Separately supported interest/fees remain financing costs; incomplete history uses supported opening obligations rather than fabricated purchases or repayments. Nonstandard financial components still require evidence before automatic posting.

## Accounts and groupings

The user rejects both current category systems as the desired target. Use [the expense-informed chart proposal](../proposals/personal-chart-of-accounts.md) as the working vocabulary, with intermediate account detail and seven grocery subaccounts, parent/group summaries, and no account per SKU. Individual ambiguous merchant/product mappings still need evidence.

- Keep accounting types—asset, liability, equity, income, expense—distinct from purpose groups.
- Q28: expense ordinary personal purchases, including ordinary durable household goods, when acquired. Use tracked assets/prepayments only for explicitly identified, evidence-supported cases; ambiguous financial nature stays in review. Automatic capitalization thresholds and general depreciation are outside the first release.
- Groups can contain several accounts across accounting types. Use one primary purpose for additive summaries and optional overlapping custom groups for other views.
- Show period spending, asset balances, debt outstanding, and repayments as separate measures. Do not sum an expense and the liability that financed it into additional spending.
- Mixed-purpose card debt needs allocation through the underlying purchase/posting relationships; whole-card membership alone cannot assign the entire balance to each purpose. Q31: follow explicit issuer repayment allocations where available; otherwise use a disclosed oldest-billed-first analytical convention. Keep unsupported opening debt unattributed and excess payments as unapplied card credits. Do not claim issuer-confirmed purchase settlement or reduce unbilled principal without evidence; attributed/unattributed components must reconcile to the card balance. Purchase expenses and the total repayment are unchanged.
- Purchases made while abroad use Travel, including meals, transport and shopping. Online subscriptions retain ordinary accounts, even when billed in foreign currency or renewed during a trip. Currency or processor nationality alone does not establish physical purchase abroad. Core travel services remain Travel by their purpose.
- The vehicle-plan line is confirmed as car payments under Transportation; Sancor covers car insurance only. Purpose confirmation does not establish vehicle ownership, loan balance, or a principal/fee/insurance breakdown.
- Directly linked refunds/promotions follow the original purchase account as distinct credits, including Travel when applicable. Unrelated rewards and uncertain tax credits remain for review.
- Reviewed items of a verified, linked domestic receipt allocate the same purchase across grocery, household, personal-care, pet, and other accounts, reconciling to its total.
- Domestic supermarket purchases without sufficient item detail remain Unallocated purchases with amount, merchant evidence, and review status visible. Do not assume Groceries or invent allocation percentages.

Maintain product identities, printed descriptions, codes, units, quantities, discounts and gross/net prices for existing product analytics. Verification of receipt arithmetic does not validate a product identity or its account classification. Public category research and private line-level review are separate; the latter remains under ignored local `qa-output/account-design/`.

## Import and correction workflow

- Automatically post records whose identity, financial nature and totals are established by the accepted rules and validation.
- Q30: a receipt may establish one purchase and supported card liability before its statement when acquisition, amount, payment method and specific card identity are supported. Retain pending statement reconciliation; never infer a financing schedule from the receipt amount alone. Later statement evidence confirms/enriches the same purchase and establishes billing. Insufficient facts or unsupported split allocations remain in review, without suppressing existing receipt analytics. Arithmetic verification alone is insufficient.
- Uncertain purpose can use Unallocated where appropriate. Ambiguous financial nature, duplicate identity or conflicting totals remains in review; no unsupported principal, funding account, or past event is invented.
- Default account corrections to the selected purchase. Provide explicit options to create/update a reusable future rule and to apply a correction to matching history, preserving the current bulk capability as an explicit action.
- Version source evidence and retain posted entries through explicit reversal/replacement with traceability. Unposted extraction drafts can remain editable. Preserve classification/product decisions, transcripts, links, and review history.
- Historical analytics use the latest corrected classification, including for old purchases. Original evidence, earlier assignments and posted-entry revisions remain inspectable in the audit history. A separate full “as originally reported” chart mode is outside the agreed scope. Q29: corrections to original facts/classifications reverse/replace in the original effective period, retaining the actual correction time and immutable audit chain. No period locks initially. New refunds and later charges retain their own evidenced event dates.

## Hosted receipt processing and retained operations

Processing PDFs within the private hosted installation is accepted. Keep exact-cent admission, rejection/correction, duplicate detection, transcript provenance, product review, and receipt-charge association behavior. Evaluate hosting-compatible OCR against the existing fixtures before choosing an engine. Permission to send documents to external OCR/AI services has not been given; this is separate from the existing optional merchant-name classification boundary.

Preserve backup/restore and document/reference retention as capabilities. PostgreSQL and hosted document storage will need corresponding backup and restore proof; the current SQLite ZIP implementation itself is replaceable. Do not treat importing existing receipt data as providing working hosted OCR.

## Multilingual preservation and new accounting features

The user's additional requirement is to keep the app multilingual as accounting features are added. Preserve English and Spanish, English as the initial/default and fallback language, and an explicit remembered language choice without an application login or automatic browser-language override. No additional language has been requested. The current implementation uses a `lang` cookie shared by server rendering and the client provider; a replacement must preserve the behavior, not necessarily that code.

Current evidence: [language definitions and translation functions](../../web/src/lib/i18n.ts), [request locale selection](../../web/src/lib/locale.ts), [language picker](../../web/src/components/LangToggle.tsx), [client provider](../../web/src/components/I18nProvider.tsx), and [root layout](../../web/src/app/layout.tsx). Existing [translation tests](../../web/src/lib/i18n.test.ts) check key parity, nonblank strings, matching placeholders, plural pairs and selected chart-label collisions. These tests were inspected, not run, and do not establish complete translation of every rendered string.

### Identity, labels and source evidence

- Accounting accounts, types, purpose groups, purchases, postings, event kinds and review/error states have stable language-independent identities. Translate their display labels at presentation time; never identify, match, join, aggregate, or allocate using translated names. Changing language or a label must not create an account, change a posting, move a purchase between groups, or alter the Q31 repayment order.
- Supply English/Spanish labels for every built-in account and purpose-group concept in the new chart, as well as account types, billed/unbilled debt, clearing roles, review states and correction explanations. Do not carry obsolete category labels into the new chart merely because their translations already exist. The vocabulary must distinguish purchases, amounts billed and actual payments consistently in both languages.
- Q32 in [the interview](../current-state/accounting-questions.md#q32-custom-account-and-group-names-across-languages) accepts one shared user-authored name for custom accounts/groups and user renames, shown verbatim in either language. A custom rename overrides the built-in display name in both languages; built-in names without an override remain fully translated. Separate per-language custom names are outside the initial scope. Do not automatically translate personal labels or treat them as immutable source-document evidence; editing a label never changes financial identity.
- Preserve original statement/receipt descriptions, merchant and product source text, tax/legal identifiers, source PDFs/transcripts, and existing evidence/audit prose. A translated explanation can accompany that evidence but must not replace it or silently translate merchant aliases, canonical product names or user notes. Language switching must not send any private text to an external translation service.
- Chart series, selectors and stored filters use stable identities even if two labels are identical in a language. Language changes preserve current account/group selections, reporting settings, navigation context and unsaved form values; where that cannot be retained safely, require explicit handling before discarding a draft.

### Complete interface and structured messages

- Include both languages for new navigation, forms, help, empty states, confirmations, validation/review messages, journal/account details, group and debt reports, chart legends/tooltips, table headings, accessibility labels, and import/correction feedback. Multilingual coverage is part of each feature's acceptance rather than a later translation phase.
- Extend the current typed-key, fallback and placeholder conventions or provide an equivalent replacement. Both supported languages must have complete nonblank catalogs and matching variables. English fallback is runtime resilience, not permission to ship missing Spanish translations. Keep whole translatable messages rather than constructing sentences from translated fragments.
- Build on the separation demonstrated by [Failure](../../web/src/lib/failure.ts): stable machine-readable error/event code, message key and structured parameters; request/client rendering selects the language. Retained new accounting/reconciliation messages must be renderable again in the current language instead of storing only the first rendered sentence. Preserve source excerpts and previously stored prose separately. Raw framework/database/worker errors need an understandable localized explanation at the user boundary.
- Preserve correct English/Spanish singular/plural handling. The current one/other rule is specific to the supported pair; additional languages would require their own plural/message rules and complete coverage. Do not add a library or new language solely for this documentation requirement.

### Financial formatting and parsing

- Interface language is independent of native currency, monetary precision, valuation mode, effective dates, cycle/period assignments and actual correction time. Switching English/Spanish changes no journal amounts, balances, exchange rates, IPC/MEP basis, reconciliation result or purchase identity.
- Preserve the current language-independent Argentine financial formatting behavior: [report formatting](../../web/src/lib/format.ts) uses Argentine ARS/percentage conventions, while USD strings currently use `US$` with two dot-decimal digits. [Receipt money handling](../../web/src/lib/receipts/money.ts) retains exact cents, Argentine printed amount/quantity parsing and Argentine peso display. Do not infer that selecting English selects US input conventions, changes currency, or authorizes rounding ledger values. New journal/reconciliation detail must expose the precision needed to explain its values in either language.
- Source-document parsing follows the document/issuer format, never the viewer's selected language. Existing receipt correction must still pass the same exact-cent gate. New manually entered financial values need a stated input format and validation into canonical exact values; any later localized-input support must parse using an explicit convention and reject ambiguous input rather than guessing. This does not request a new regional-format preference.
- Translate human-readable calendar/report captions while retaining unambiguous underlying dates and period identities. Language selection alone must not change timezone or shift an event to another economic period; hosting timezone configuration remains engineering work under the accepted date policies.

Future verification should cover catalog/type checks, interpolation/plurals, missing-key fallback, translated built-in account/group labels, matching errors rendered in both languages from the same retained code/parameters, and an integration walkthrough of accounting/receipt/correction flows in English and Spanish. Compare stable IDs, journal amounts, allocations, totals, dates, valuation settings and selected context before/after switching language. Include raw-evidence preservation, exact-cent receipt correction, label collisions and longer Spanish labels on narrow screens. Verify Q32 by switching languages before/after a custom rename: built-in names translate, the custom label stays verbatim, and selected IDs, amounts and group memberships stay unchanged. Existing [receipt category tests](../../web/src/lib/receipts/categories.test.ts) are an example of checking labels for every domain identifier, not a requirement to retain the old taxonomy. No application tests or browser checks were run for this documentation addition.
